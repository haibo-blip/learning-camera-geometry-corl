\begin{abstract}
RGB visuomotor policies can train from ordinary images, but often learn
view-specific shortcuts that weaken when camera placement changes.
Geometry-aware policies expose rays, pose encodings, or point clouds, but
typically require calibrated extrinsics, depth, or 3D-specific encoders, leaving
many uncalibrated, depth-free demonstrations unusable for geometry-aware
learning.  We learn camera geometry end-to-end inside the policy.  A pose head
predicts extrinsics from RGB, differentiable Plucker ray construction conditions
the action expert, and ground-truth poses supervise only a subset of episodes.
Unlabeled episodes still train through action loss, and in our NVS variant also
through target-view reconstruction from predicted-pose queries.

This formulation combines RGB data accessibility with explicit geometric
conditioning.  On LIBERO-MV Spatial, our coupled camera-pose policy matches or
exceeds policies conditioned on ground-truth camera rays or relative pose,
despite predicting geometry from RGB.  On MimicGen, NVS improves over the
action-only camera-pose policy by 13.1 train-camera points and 23.1 tight-camera
points, and is competitive with a point-cloud policy without using depth.  On
real-world tabletop tasks, the NVS policy outperforms a decoupled
calibration-free baseline and exceeds the calibrated Plucker-GT aggregate while
using pose labels for only half of training episodes and no calibration at
inference.
\end{abstract}
