This paper studies camera geometry as a learned component of visuomotor control rather than as fixed calibration metadata.  The action-only variant tests whether partial pose supervision and action loss can jointly train a camera-aware policy, including from episodes without camera-extrinsic labels.  The NVS variant adds a view-synthesis objective that regularizes latent scene tokens through held-out view reconstruction, and is the stronger variant in the current MimicGen camera-shift results.  Together, these variants suggest a path toward camera-aware robot policies that learn geometry inside the control objective while retaining training signal from demonstrations that do not provide full calibration.
